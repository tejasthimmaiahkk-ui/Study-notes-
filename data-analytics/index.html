Data Analytics

From "what is analytics?" to computing correlations, testing hypotheses and building a Power BI dashboard. The statistics is taught from first principles (mean, deviation, Σ notation) with fully worked tables; every formula has a calculator that shows its steps.

Exam: 3 hoursMax marks 100 (Exam 80 + IA 20)56 teaching hours

Units

Unit I · 14 hours · CO1

Introduction to Data Analytics

Evolution, overview, descriptive/diagnostic/predictive/prescriptive analytics, importance, business applications, text analytics, web analytics and the skills an analyst needs.
Unit II · 14 hours · CO2

Correlation & Regression

Types of correlation, Karl Pearson's coefficient (three methods), probable error, Spearman's rank correlation (with ties), regression lines, coefficients and their properties.
Unit III · 14 hours · CO3

Probability, Distributions & Testing

Sample space, events, probability rules, conditional probability, Bayes' theorem, random variables, Binomial, Poisson, Normal, estimation, t-test, ANOVA and Chi-square.
Unit IV · 14 hours · CO4

Power BI & Business Intelligence

BI concepts, Power BI Desktop/Service/Mobile, interface, data sources, Power Query cleaning, merge & append, visuals, dashboards, slicers, drill-through and bookmarks.
Exam kit

Revision & Exam Kit

Formula sheet, statistical tables, decision flowchart for choosing a test, likely questions and a self-test.

How to score in this paper

  • Always draw the working table with column headings (X, Y, x, y, x², y², xy). Most marks are for the table.
  • Write the formula first, then substitute, then compute.
  • For tests, write the five steps: H₀, H₁, test statistic, table value at α and df, decision in words.
  • Round only at the end (keep 4 decimal places in between).
  • Use headings and a diagram (analytics types chart, BI architecture, Power BI workflow).
  • Give an example from business for every type or feature.
  • For Power BI questions, write the exact steps and menu names (Home → Transform data → …).
