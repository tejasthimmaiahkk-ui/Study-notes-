Timed mock test (20 questions · 30 minutes)

Press Start, solve on paper, click your option. The score updates as you go. Don't open explanations until the timer ends!

1. LCM of 12, 18 and 30 is:

  1. 90
  2. 120
  3. 180
  4. 360

2² × 3² × 5 = 180.

2. 15 % of 80 % of 500 =

  1. 60
  2. 75
  3. 40
  4. 80

0.15 × 400.

3. A sells to B at 20 % profit; B sells to C at 10 % profit. C pays ₹264. A's cost price:

  1. ₹220
  2. ₹240
  3. ₹180
  4. ₹200

264/(1.2 × 1.1) = 200.

4. Two numbers are in the ratio 3 : 5 and their sum is 64. The larger is:

  1. 24
  2. 40
  3. 36
  4. 45

64 × 5/8.

5. Average of the first 10 odd numbers:

  1. 9
  2. 11
  3. 10
  4. 10.5

Sum n² = 100; ÷ 10.

6. A 200 m train at 72 km/h crosses a 300 m bridge in:

  1. 25 s
  2. 20 s
  3. 15 s
  4. 30 s

500/20.

7. A can do a job in 15 days and B in 10 days. Together:

  1. 5 days
  2. 6 days
  3. 7.5 days
  4. 12.5 days

Total 30; 2 + 3 = 5/day.

8. A pipe fills a tank in 4 h; a leak empties it in 12 h. Time to fill with the leak:

  1. 3 h
  2. 8 h
  3. 5 h
  4. 6 h

1/4 − 1/12 = 1/6.

9. Roots of x² − 9x + 20 = 0:

  1. 2, 10
  2. −4, −5
  3. 4, 5
  4. 1, 20

Product 20, sum 9.

10. Unit digit of 13⁴⁷:

  1. 7
  2. 3
  3. 9
  4. 1

47 mod 4 = 3 → 3, 9, 7 → 7.

11. A committee of 2 men and 1 woman from 5 men and 4 women:

  1. 20
  2. 40
  3. 60
  4. 84

⁵C₂ × ⁴C₁ = 10 × 4.

12. Arrangements of the letters of COMPUTER:

  1. 5,040
  2. 20,160
  3. 720
  4. 40,320

8 distinct letters: 8!.

13. P(a king or a queen) from a pack of 52:

  1. 2/13
  2. 1/13
  3. 1/26
  4. 4/13

8/52.

14. log₁₀ 0.01 =

  1. 2
  2. −1
  3. −2
  4. 0.01

10⁻² = 0.01.

15. The 10th term of 2, 5, 8, … is:

  1. 32
  2. 29
  3. 30
  4. 27

2 + 9 × 3.

16. 1 + 3 + 9 + … (6 terms) =

  1. 243
  2. 729
  3. 121
  4. 364

(3⁶ − 1)/2.

17. Area of a circle whose circumference is 44 cm:

  1. 154 cm²
  2. 616 cm²
  3. 77 cm²
  4. 308 cm²

r = 7.

18. Volume of a cylinder with r = 7 cm, h = 4 cm:

  1. 154 cm³
  2. 308 cm³
  3. 616 cm³
  4. 1232 cm³

22/7 × 49 × 4.

19. Successive discounts of 10 % and 20 % equal a single discount of:

  1. 30 %
  2. 28 %
  3. 25 %
  4. 32 %

10 + 20 − 2.

20. A invests ₹6,000 for 12 months, B ₹8,000 for 6 months. Of a ₹5,000 profit A gets:

  1. ₹3,000
  2. ₹2,000
  3. ₹2,500
  4. ₹3,500

72,000 : 48,000 = 3 : 2.
