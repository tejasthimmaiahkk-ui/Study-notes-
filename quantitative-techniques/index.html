How to use these notes

  1. Read the idea and the standard method; follow each worked example with pen and paper.
  2. Learn the shortcut only after you understand why it works.
  3. Do the practice questions timed (aim for about 1 minute each), then check the answer.
  4. Use the calculators to generate your own examples and check your work.
  5. Before the exam: formula sheet + mock test on the revision page.
